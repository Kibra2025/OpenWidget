OpenWidget - third-party components
===================================

OpenWidget's MIT license covers its original code and assets only.
The following unmodified binaries retain their upstream licenses.
Exact runtime binary SHA-256 values are recorded in dependencies.json.
The GPL driver installer is a separate, optional program, not linked into
OpenWidget. No DualSenseClient application or assemblies are distributed.
